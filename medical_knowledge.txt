Q: What are the common symptoms of influenza (flu)?
A: Common symptoms include high fever, chills, persistent dry cough, sore throat, runny or stuffy nose, muscle aches, severe fatigue, and headache. Staying hydrated and getting adequate rest are critical.

Q: How can I treat a minor first-degree burn at home?
A: Immediately cool the burn under running cool tap water for 10 to 15 minutes. Never apply ice directly. Apply pure aloe vera gel or petroleum jelly, and loosely cover with a clean sterile gauze bandage.

Q: What are the primary symptoms of Covid-19?
A: Primary symptoms include fever or chills, dry cough, shortness of breath, sudden loss of taste or smell, persistent fatigue, and general body aches.

Q: What steps should be taken for seasonal allergy prevention?
A: Minimize outdoor exposure during high-pollen morning hours, keep windows closed, run HEPA air purifiers indoors, and wash your face and hands after coming inside.
