Q: What are the primary symptoms of Covid-19?
A: Primary symptoms include fever or chills, dry cough, shortness of breath, fatigue, and body aches.

Q: What steps should be taken for seasonal allergy prevention?
A: Minimize outdoor exposure during high-pollen morning hours, keep windows closed, and use HEPA air purifiers.

Q: What are common symptoms of influenza (flu)?
A: High fever, chills, persistent dry cough, sore throat, runny nose, and severe muscle fatigue.

Q: How can I treat a minor first-degree burn at home?
A: Cool under running tap water for 10 to 15 minutes. Never apply ice directly. Apply aloe vera gel or petroleum jelly.
